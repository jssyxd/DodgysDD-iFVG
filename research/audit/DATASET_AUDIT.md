### 6. Execution consistency

* the standalone expectancy over every resolved candidate is **+0.0222 R** (n=366,486).
* **the label is NOT the executed result.** `r_multiple` is the outcome *as if the candidate were the only open position*; the backtest trades **one position at a time** and **drops** the signals that arrive while a position is open. The portfolio's trade set is a strict subset, and its expectancy differs from the number above. An ML model trained on `label` therefore learns a different problem from the one the portfolio solves — this is by design (D4), but it must be stated.
* `confirm_bar` is monotonic in the file: yes (the log is written in time order)
